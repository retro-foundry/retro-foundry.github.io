Slam Tilt

Drop the ADF for the table you want to play, or choose it from your device.

  • Disk 2 Mean Machines
  • Disk 3 The Pirate
  • Disk 4 Ace of Space
  • Disk 5 Night of the Demon

Touch either side to flip. Flick to nudge. Hold anywhere to launch. Use browser Back to change tables.

F1 starts a game, Enter launches, left and right Ctrl flip, and Space nudges. Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen.

With a gamepad, press Start/Options to start a game. Triggers or bumpers flip, A/Cross launches, X/Square nudges, and Y/Triangle pauses.

Rotate your device upright to play Slam Tilt.
