For more projects use the top left links (GitHub, Discord, or Patreon).

Remote multiplayer: Copy the join link from the top right and send it to a friend. They open the link to watch the host stream and play as player two; remote keyboard and gamepads always control player two.

Fullscreen: Press F11 to toggle browser fullscreen.

Guest controls: WASD or arrows move/turn, Q/E strafe, Space or Ctrl fires. A connected gamepad also works.

Connection is peer-to-peer and may not work behind strict corporate firewalls or some networks.
