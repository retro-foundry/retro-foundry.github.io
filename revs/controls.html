Wheel and controller setup

Set up a gamepad, wheel, separate pedals, or sequential gearstick. Changes take effect only when you choose Apply. The game and audio are held while this page is open.

Connecting to Revs…

Keep the game tab focused and press a physical controller button, then release it, if your browser has not exposed the device. Use your wheel's PC/USB mode.

    Steering / gamepad

    Centre the wheel or stick before choosing Learn. Then turn fully right first and fully left.

    
  
    Pedals
    Release the pedal before choosing Learn, then press it fully. Choose either an axis or a button for throttle and brake.
    
  
    Gearstick / paddles
    Gear up and gear down feed Revs' sequential gearbox. Direct H-pattern gear selection is not supported.
    
  
    Assists
    Automatic gears is a modern input assist. Auto clutch controls whether device shifts require the clutch. Steering assist selects the original CAS option.
